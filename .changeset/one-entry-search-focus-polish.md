**What changed.** The form pane, when it takes focus after Enter opens an entry with no fields, draws the editor's
focus ring inside its edges. A tree row's cut-off line shows in full below the row on keyboard
focus, after the same delay as on hover, so it no longer covers the row's title. The field's
shortcut hint takes the placeholder's colour, and its clear buttons read "Clear search".
