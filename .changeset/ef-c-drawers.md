### Compact drawer headers, clickable comment cards and a steadier owner chip

<!-- migration
area: Editing
action: None
row: Drawers share the content panel's compact header
-->

**What changed.** The Branches, Comments, Media and staged-changes drawers take the content panel's compact header with no subtitle. A thread card in the Comments drawer jumps like its Jump button. The Branches drawer no longer offers "New branch comment", since new branch comments start in the Comments drawer. A person's chip keeps its avatar and a long name on one line.
